## CHARACTER GUIDE — SEASON 1

### THE FOLAMI HOUSEHOLD
- **MRS. ADENIKE FOLAMI, 44** — The tenant. Proud, stubborn, dangerously skilled at making you feel guilty for her own wrongdoing. Her financial collapse came from her husband abandoning her. Instead of confessing to her landlord she chose silence, pride, and manipulation.
- **MR. SEGUN FOLAMI, 47** — Adenike's estranged husband who quietly returned under the cover of overcrowding. Owes money to dangerous people.
- **TOBILOBA FOLAMI, 22** — Eldest daughter. University dropout running a secret Instagram boutique. Most self-aware member of the family. Her journal entries serve as the series voiceover.
- **KENNY FOLAMI, 17** — The son. Streetwise, charismatic, slowly being recruited by area boys.
- **MAMA RISI, 60** — Adenike's sister. Large, loud, considers herself co-landlord of wherever she sleeps.
- **BABA RISI, 63** — Mama Risi's husband. Mostly silent, mostly drunk.
- **FEMI & DOTUN** — Two cousins, early 30s, unemployed.
- **BLESSING, 26** — Young woman of unexplained relationship to the Folamis.

### THE LANDLORD SIDE
- **MRS. RONKE ALUKO, 58** — The landlord. Retired civil servant who sank her entire pension and husband's life insurance into building 3 Joyin Street.
- **BARRISTER SEUN ALUKO, 31** — Ronke's son. Young lawyer, aggressive, carries guilt for not helping build the house.
- **MR. LEKAN ALUKO, 62** — Ronke's husband. Mild stroke survivor. His medical care depends on the rental income.

### THE COMMUNITY
- **MAMA TUNDE, 51** — Next-door neighbour. Street historian and chief gossip.
- **CHIEF ADEWALE, 66** — Community elder who believes all disputes should be settled at his table first.
- **OFFICER BIODUN KAZEEM, 35** — Police officer who responded to the April 2025 incident.
- **PASTOR DELE OGUNSANYA, 48** — Church pastor three buildings down.
- **AMAKA, 28** — Hairdresser who rents the small shop attached to the compound fence.

## EPISODE 3: "Nine Shoes at the Door"

---

### SCENE 1 — THE ARRIVAL OF SEUN

```
[IMAGE PROMPT]
Saturday morning confrontation inside a modest Nigerian parlour.
Seated across a low centre table: MRS. ALUKO (58, coral lace,
reading glasses, notepad on her knee) and BARRISTER SEUN ALUKO
(31, fitted navy agbada, his own notepad, jaw set). On the opposite
side: MRS. FOLAMI (44, ankara boubou, hair freshly done, cold drinks
tray between them she has just set down). The parlour has framed
scriptures on the wall, heavy curtains, a TV on mute.
```

```
[VIDEO PROMPT]
Open on the parlour — cold drinks already sweating on the tray.
Mrs. Folami pours. Seun declines. Mrs. Aluko accepts to maintain
social form. Camera moves slowly between both sides of the table —
a tennis match of composed aggression.
```

```
[CAMERA]
Shot 1: Wide — full parlour, three figures at the table
Shot 2: Close — the sweating cold drinks on the tray
Shot 3: Medium two-shot — Seun and Mrs. Aluko across the table
Shot 4: Single close — Mrs. Folami pouring, gracious smile
Shot 5: Alternating close-ups during dialogue
Lens: 50mm and 85mm alternating | Movement: Very slow push during most tense exchange
```

```
[DIALOGUE]

(Mrs. Folami sets down the tray. She sits with the ease of a
woman hosting a social call, not an interrogation.)

MRS. FOLAMI:
I made zobo this morning. Cold. You are welcome.

(Seun shakes his head slightly. Mrs. Aluko takes a glass.)

MRS. ALUKO:
(pleasantly, pen poised)
How is everyone managing in the space?
It seems a little full.

MRS. FOLAMI:
(laughing gently)
Ah, Mama! Family is family.
My sister came when I wasn't well —
she brought her people to help.
They are going soon.

SEUN:
(dry, level)
The agreement says two adults.

MRS. FOLAMI:
(turning to him — smile doesn't waver)
Seun, you are a lawyer.
You understand that situations change.
We are adjusting to life.

SEUN:
You are also adjusting the rent payments?

(Silence. The cold drink sweats on the tray.
Mrs. Aluko watches her son.
Mrs. Folami holds Seun's gaze without blinking.)

(CRASH from the kitchen — a tray dropping.
MAMA RISI erupts through the kitchen door
carrying puff-puff on a plate, laughing loudly about nothing.)

MAMA RISI:
(enormous, cheerful, filling the room)
Ah! You people are sitting here dry-dry!
Barrister! Take puff-puff! My hand made this!

(The tension breaks. Seun blinks.
Mama Risi deposits the plate and holds court.
Mrs. Folami, behind Mama Risi's back, exhales one breath of relief.
Tobiloba, watching from the hallway, sees this.
She turns and goes back to her room.)
```

[IMAGE PROMPT]
A mid-range restaurant in Ikeja — warm lighting, wooden tables.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Voiceover recording:** Tobiloba's narration — quiet room, close microphone, conversational not theatrical. She is writing in her journal, not performing. Mrs. Folami's single narration (S3 Ep3) records separately — slower, more interior.

**Music:** A single Afro-piano motif runs through all three seasons — sparse in Season 1, fragmented in Season 2, resolved in Season 3.

**The gate:** Every season opens and closes on the gate. The gate IS the series.

**Colour grade across seasons:**
- S1 — Warm amber building to flat cool by Episode 10
- S2 — Cooler throughout, warmth only in Mr. Lekan scenes
- S3 — Warm re-emerging, fully arrived by the finale

**The flower bed:** Always in frame when Mrs. Aluko is in the compound. Always in its current state — dead, overgrown, or blooming. It is the property's honest face.
